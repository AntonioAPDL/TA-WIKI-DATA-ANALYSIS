\section*{Purpose of this review}

This descriptive departmental survey evaluation concerns graduate-student reports of
awareness, use, contribution barriers, and perceived value of a collaboratively
maintained TA Wiki. The immediate purpose of coauthor review is to confirm the
v0.4 scientific scope, restricted-rerun path, and finalization sequence before
any restricted result is considered for release. The study is descriptive and
does not support causal claims, an unverified response rate, or population-level
generalization.

\section*{What is ready now}

The tracked repository contains a reproducible, privacy-protected workflow for
restricted intake, validation, transformation, structured analysis, qualitative
workspace setup, internal scientific review, disclosure-controlled candidate
generation, attestation
verification, and synthetic testing. A historic immutable source freeze,
structured lineage, and disclosure-controlled candidate exist in approved
restricted storage. The revised v0.4 baseline has not yet been run, so that
historic candidate is audit context rather than a result-bearing input.

The survey-only project has an institutional exemption determination. Its
approved recruitment plan limited outreach to general announcements to eligible
graduate students and required separate raffle/contact handling. The verified
study record must still establish the implemented recruitment details. The
determination does not itself establish the approved restricted-data workspace,
release decision, or final manuscript declarations. The controlled reporting
policy currently sets a minimum public cell count of five.

Earlier restricted runs remain historic context only. The future v0.4 candidate
will be bound to its frozen clean analysis baseline and will reject a mixed or
stale validation--transformation--analysis chain. Manuscript-build provenance
is recorded separately for traceability, but a later checkout is not
automatically authorized to handle restricted artifacts.

\section*{Evidence boundary}

The repository may be reviewed for code, documentation, controlled metadata,
synthetic tests, and safe workflow design. Restricted source data, timestamps,
respondent-level derivatives, open text, internal tables, governance evidence,
and release candidates remain outside version control. Coauthor scientific
review may occur in the approved restricted environment; sharing an aggregate
artifact beyond that boundary requires disclosure review, attestation, and a
separate delivery decision.
