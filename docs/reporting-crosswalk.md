# Reporting crosswalk for the TA Wiki survey study

**Use:** copy this source-safe template to the approved restricted review
workspace and complete the evidence/status columns there. Do not add survey
values, source locators, approval identifiers, reviewer names, or candidate
hashes to this tracked copy.

## How to use the crosswalk

1. Treat CROSS as the primary survey-reporting checklist.
2. Use STROBE to sharpen cross-sectional reporting and limitations.
3. Apply CHERRIES only to online-survey details that can be verified from the
   authoritative study record.
4. Use AAPOR terminology only where sample/disposition information supports
   it. Do not manufacture a response rate from an unknown or incomplete
   denominator.
5. Create a claim/evidence row before adding or retaining a result,
   interpretation, contextual fact, or declaration in the final manuscript.

The [manuscript finalization evidence matrix](../manuscript/finalization-evidence-matrix.md)
is the claim-level companion to this checklist.

## Core whole-report crosswalk

| Reporting element | Manuscript location | Evidence needed before final wording | Current safe status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title and structured abstract | Title page; abstract | Author-approved title; approved findings for empirical statements; verified setting and scope. | Working title, noncausal scope, and the approved structured result may be drafted; final title and venue form need author review. |
| Research purpose and objectives | Introduction | Verified project purpose and author-approved research objectives/questions. | Descriptive purpose is available; final questions need author review. |
| Design and setting | Methods | Verified study mode, setting, fielding period, and context. | Do not infer details from a draft instrument or email summary. |
| Eligibility, recruitment, and sample frame | Methods | Verified eligibility screen, outreach routes, target group, and invitation/disposition information. | State the voluntary departmental scope; do not state a response rate without verified support. |
| Instrument and measures | Methods; supplement if appropriate | Frozen source/context confirmation of analyzed wording, options, routing, and pretesting information. | Controlled metadata supports the analysis structure; live-form wording remains a restricted verification task. |
| Governance, consent, data protection, and incentive | Methods; declarations | Exact authoritative wording and applicable institutional/study records. | High-level boundary is known; no identifier or unverified claim belongs in the public repository. |
| Data preparation and missingness | Methods; Results | Controlled transformation rules, item-specific denominator rule, anomaly decisions, and any sensitivity treatment. | Pipeline documents the rules; final claim boundaries await restricted scientific review. |
| Descriptive analysis | Methods; Results | Approved candidate artifact for every numerical statement/table; no causal or population-generalization wording. | Current v0.4 candidate and author-review manuscript build exist in restricted storage; the historic candidate is audit context only. |
| Exploratory analyses | Methods; Results or supplement | Predeclared pairs, pairwise completeness, clear exploratory label, and separate disclosure assessment. | Keep internal unless separately reviewed and approved. |
| Participant flow and descriptive data | Results | Approved cohort/item-completeness artifact; missingness handling and denominators. | Do not report counts/percentages from memory or internal outputs. |
| Limitations and interpretation | Discussion | Verified recruitment/context facts and approved findings. | Retain voluntary, small-sample, descriptive, and disclosure-boundary cautions where supported. |
| Generalizability | Discussion | Explicit target/sampling frame and defensible evidence. | Avoid population-wide or causal claims. |
| Funding, conflicts, CRediT, acknowledgments, availability | Declarations | Author-approved, verified records and venue requirements. | Placeholders only until confirmed. |

## STROBE cross-sectional items

| STROBE item(s) | Required action | Evidence route |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3: title, abstract, rationale, objectives | Name the design accurately and make objectives explicit. | Author review; verified project framing. |
| 4–6: design, setting, participants | State setting/dates, eligibility, selection, and any recruitment limitations. | Restricted study-context record. |
| 7–11: variables, sources, bias, study size, quantitative handling | Define items, coding, denominator rules, missingness, and sensitivity handling; acknowledge self-selection/coverage limitations. | Controlled metadata, analysis specification, restricted methods review. |
| 12: statistical methods | Describe descriptive summaries and the absence of unplanned inferential or causal claims; state any sensitivity analysis. | Controlled analysis code/specification. |
| 13–15: participants and descriptive/outcome data | Provide an approved flow/completeness account and item-specific denominators. | Exact disclosure-approved candidate only. |
| 16: other analyses | Mark cross-tabs or sensitivity results as exploratory/supporting; do not imply confirmation. | Predeclared scope plus scientific/disclosure review. |
| 17–21: key results, limitations, interpretation, generalizability | Separate observed patterns from interpretation; do not extend beyond the responding group and verified context. | Claim/evidence matrix and coauthor review. |
| 22: funding | Use the venue's required disclosure wording. | Author-verified declaration record. |

## CHERRIES items, if the verified study record confirms an e-survey

| CHERRIES topic | What to record or state | Guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Design and target/sample frame | Open/closed status, target group, and likely self-selection boundary. | Do not call a convenience or volunteer sample representative. |
| IRB/exemption, consent, data protection | Exact governance status, consent process, stored data types, and protection boundary. | Use verified institutional wording only. |
| Development and pretesting | Instrument development, pilot/usability/technical testing if documented. | Say "not documented" rather than infer. |
| Recruitment/contact/advertising | Channels, general vs individual invitation, and announcement wording if retained. | Do not reconstruct details from memory. |
| Administration | Survey mode, context, voluntary nature, incentives, dates, routing, mandatory fields, review step, and duplicate prevention. | Report only features verified in the authoritative record. |
| Completion and response-process metrics | View, participation, completion, or completeness measures only if numerators and denominators are defined and captured. | Avoid a generic "response rate" if the disposition basis is unknown. |
| Incomplete and atypical submissions | Prespecified handling, data-quality rules, and any timestamp review. | Keep timestamps and respondent-level audits restricted. |

## AAPOR decision rule for rates

Before using *response rate*, *cooperation rate*, *completion rate*, or similar
terminology, complete all of the following in restricted review:

- identify the sampled/invited unit and its eligibility rule;
- account for every relevant final disposition or explain the unknown cases;
- specify the numerator and denominator with the applicable AAPOR convention;
- verify that the calculation matches the administered survey mode; and
- determine whether the number would meaningfully characterize a voluntary,
  nonprobability departmental survey.

If any of these are unavailable, report only the approved number of analyzed
responses or item-specific valid-response denominator as appropriate, together
with an explicit limitation. Do not substitute a raffle-entry count, page view,
or informal mailing-list size.

## Result artifact and presentation checklist

For every table, figure, or numerical sentence proposed for inclusion:

- [ ] It maps to an exact disclosure-approved candidate artifact.
- [ ] Its denominator and missingness rule are defined in the caption, note,
  surrounding text, or referenced methods statement.
- [ ] It has an appropriate title/caption, human-readable label, and a clear
  note for suppression or withheld values.
- [ ] It does not combine separately released values in a way that exposes a
  suppressed cell or rare combination.
- [ ] It is not a screenshot or manually retyped internal result.
- [ ] Its interpretation is descriptive, noncausal, and bounded by the sample
  and verified context.
- [ ] It has passed the claim/evidence and author-review checks.
